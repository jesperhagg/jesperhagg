### What I'm building

#### Heimdal - structured canvas workspace for strategy work
AI assisted coding apps like claude code, cursor and codex have enabled engineers to produce at a significantly higher pace than ever before, but those interfaces are adapted to engineers.
My hypothesis is that product strategy is the new bottleneck and that target user is often different from engineers, and I am betting on a post-chat AI native experience for that user group.

#### Sagokraft - Engaging and pedagogical reading experience for children
- Kids reading apps are passive and static - Sagokraft lets kids be part of creating the stories while the content supports their development through age adapted language progression.
- Retention through progressive user-character context that builds emotional bond.
- Market research -> Prototyping

#### Vor - Regulatory compliance automation for small-scale forestry
- Every forestry owner in Sweden need to send in a felling notice before a harvest, and small-scale forest owners spend a lot of time or money on this. There is currently no tools to support this process, it is completely manual and a struggle to navigate compliance.
- Fellingpal automates and supports this flow to reduce complexity and time spent for forestry owners and improve unit economics for forestry consultants.
- Opportunity discovery stage.
